WRITTEN SUBMISSIONS ON BEHALF OF THE APPELLANT

(Demo brief for OrderOrder. Every citation points at a judgment held in the
knowledge base. Six of the eight are wrong in a different way, and each way is a
numbered failure mode from docs/PRD.md. The expected verdicts are recorded in
demo/expected.md.)

I. IMPLEADMENT IN A SUIT FOR SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE

1. It is submitted at the outset that the appellant cannot be impleaded as a
defendant in a suit for specific performance of a contract to which he is not a
party, as held in 2019 INSC 770, para 7.

2. A subsequent purchaser who holds a prior agreement to sell is a necessary
party to the suit and must be impleaded to protect his interest, as this Court
held in 2019 INSC 770, para 3.2.

3. This Court has further held that the controversies between the parties to the
litigation alone are to be gone into under Order 1 Rule 10 CPC: see 2019 INSC
770, para 73.

4. The proposition is settled beyond argument by the decision of this Court in
Mohanlal Sharma v. State of Rajasthan, (2023) 7 SCC 4412.

II. SEIZURE AND THE NARCOTIC DRUGS AND PSYCHOTROPIC SUBSTANCES ACT, 1985

5. Procedural illegality in conducting a search and seizure does not render the
evidence collected thereby inadmissible, as this Court held in 2025 INSC 1045,
para 29.

6. It is settled that non-compliance with Section 52A of the Act vitiates the
trial in every case, as laid down in Narcotics Control Bureau v. Kashif, (2025) 3
SCC 118.

III. TOLLS AND CONCESSION AGREEMENTS

7. Articles 13 and 14 of a concession agreement permitting the levy of user fees
in perpetuity are bad in law, as this Court held in 2024 INSC 1027, para 3.

IV. ABUSE OF PROCESS

8. Repeated and frivolous litigation wastes the court's valuable time and
resources, and this Court has condemned litigants who pollute the streams of
justice: 2024 INSC 1051, para 18.
