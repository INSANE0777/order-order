court: In the Supreme Court of India
cause: Civil Appeal No. 4521 of 2025
parties: Ashok Kumar versus Union of India and others
for: the Appellant
counsel: Settled by counsel before filing

# synopsis
The appellant seeks specific performance of an agreement to sell dated 12 March 2019. The suit was
dismissed on the ground that a subsequent purchaser had not been impleaded, and the appellant says
that objection was taken by a party who had no standing to take it.

# dates
12 March 2019 - the agreement to sell was executed
04 August 2021 - the suit for specific performance was instituted
19 January 2024 - the trial court dismissed the suit for non-joinder
12 November 2024 - the High Court dismissed the first appeal

# issue Whether the suit was liable to be dismissed for non-joinder of the subsequent purchaser
A subsequent purchaser who holds a prior agreement to sell is a necessary party to a suit for specific performance.
The plaintiff is dominus litis and cannot be compelled to add a party against whom he does not want to fight.

# issue Whether the delay in approaching the writ court was fatal
Delay and laches cannot be applied strictly to a writ petition invoking public interest jurisdiction.

# prayer
allow the appeal and set aside the judgment of the High Court dated 12 November 2024
restore the suit to the file of the trial court for decision on the merits
